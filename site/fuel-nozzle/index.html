Fuel nozzles: automatic vs. manual, sizes, and which fits your pump

Written by a fuel-system technician. Updated October 30, 2026.

Pick a nozzle by three things: automatic or manual, an inlet size that matches your hose (3/4 or 1 inch), and the right spout for your fuel. Automatic nozzles shut off when the tank is full; manual ones flow until you let go. Diesel nozzles usually have a 15/16-inch spout and a green cover, and unleaded gasoline nozzles a 13/16-inch spout, which helps keep the wrong fuel out of a vehicle (Dultmeier).

Match the nozzle to your pump's flow, too. A typical 3/4-inch automatic nozzle is rated to about 14.5 gallons per minute (Dultmeier); Fill-Rite's 1-inch nozzles are rated to 40 (Fill-Rite).

Automatic vs. manual

An automatic nozzle shuts itself off when fuel covers a small hole near the tip of the spout. A manual nozzle has no shutoff: it flows as long as the handle is squeezed.

How the automatic shutoff works. While fuel flows, air is drawn in through the hole at the spout tip. When the fuel level rises and covers that hole, the air stops, a vacuum builds inside the nozzle, and it pulls a diaphragm that trips the valve closed (PEI). That's why a nozzle clicks off when the tank is full, and also why splash-back can trip it early.

Hold-open latches. Many automatic nozzles have a clip that holds the handle open at a set flow; Fill-Rite's have three latch positions (Fill-Rite). Some sites can't use them: OPW says not to use its 11A with a hold-open device at pre-pay self-service sites (OPW). Never latch a manual nozzle open.

FAQ

Can I use a gas nozzle for diesel?

It will work if it's rated for diesel, but use a diesel nozzle with the green cover and 15/16-inch spout. The different sizes and colors help prevent putting the wrong fuel in a vehicle (Dultmeier).

Why won't my automatic nozzle work on my gravity-fed farm tank?

Automatic nozzles need pumped flow to work the shutoff. Fill-Rite says its automatic nozzles aren't meant for gravity systems (Fill-Rite). On a gravity tank, use a manual nozzle and stay with it.
