Underground storage tanks

Underground storage tanks (USTs) sit at gas stations, fleet yards, airports, and plenty of small businesses. The federal UST rule (40 CFR Part 280) sets what has to be checked and how often, and your state program enforces it. These guides cover each piece of equipment.

What gets checked, and how often

Most UST compliance runs on three clocks: every 30 days, every year, and every 3 years.

How oftenWhatGuide
Every 30 daysWalkthrough inspection, including spill buckets (EPA)Walkthrough checklist
Every yearWalkthrough of containment sumps and hand-held release detection equipment (40 CFR 280.36)Walkthrough checklist
Every yearLine leak detector test on pressurized piping, plus a line tightness test unless you monitor monthly (EPA)Line leak detectors
Every 3 yearsSpill bucket tightness test (single-wall) (40 CFR 280.35)Spill buckets
Every 3 yearsOverfill prevention inspection (EPA)Overfill prevention

The federal rule also covers release detection for the tanks themselves, containment sumps, corrosion protection, and operator training. Guides on those are coming.

Your state runs the program. Most states adopted the federal rule and inspect against it, sometimes with their own forms and extra requirements. Michigan, for example, adopted the federal testing schedule (Mich. Admin. Code R 29.2120). Check with your state UST program.
