Privacy policy

Last updated October 28, 2026

FuelStorageGuide is a reading site. There are no accounts, logins, comments, or forms.

What is collected

  • Visit statistics. The site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views and see which guides are useful. It doesn't use cookies or track you across other sites.
  • Server logs. The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which processes standard request information such as IP addresses to deliver pages and protect against attacks. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • Email. If you email us, your message and address are used only to reply.

Fonts

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the fonts are downloaded. See Google's privacy policy.

Affiliate links

Some pages link to products through affiliate programs, including Amazon Associates. If you click one, the retailer may set its own cookies to credit the referral. Their privacy policies apply on their sites. See the affiliate disclosure.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the date above will change. Questions: contact@fuelstorageguide.com.
